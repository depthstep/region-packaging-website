Product Page | 118 | Custom mailer boxes are self-locking shipping boxes made from a single sheet of corrugated fiberboard, folded into a sturdy base with wings that tuck and lock closed without tape. The classic style is the Roll End Front Tuck, known as REFT, which uses dust flaps and side locks to hold its shape and cushion your product on its journey. They are the go-to choice for e-commerce fulfillment, subscription box programs and direct-to-consumer brands because they are light, strong and designed to be opened. Unlike a plain brown carton, a custom printed mailer box is a branding surface. Every panel, inside and out, can carry your logo, colours, artwork and message, turning a routine delivery into a memorable unboxing experience. Durable corrugated mailers travel safely through USPS and other carriers, arrive looking considered, and give customers a reason to share your packaging. Because they ship flat and assemble in seconds, mailer boxes are efficient to store and quick to pack, which matters when you fulfil orders by hand or through a fulfilment partner. The front tuck and side locks mean no tape is needed, so packing is faster and the finished parcel looks clean. From a single unit to a full subscription box run, the same reliable structure scales with your business.
Product Page | 118 | How your box is printed shapes both the look and the unit cost. For short runs and photographic artwork, full-colour digital printing is ideal because there are no plates to pay for. For large, repeating orders, flexographic printing brings the per-unit cost down using spot colours. Whichever method fits, we print your custom printed mailer boxes to a consistent standard and can brand every panel, inside and out.
